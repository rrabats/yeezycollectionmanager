# Catalog review: draft 1

79 shoes: 73 × 350 V2 and 6 × 350 V2 CMPCT. General and regional releases only, plus the unreleased MX Frost Blue.

**How to review:** check the list for missing shoes and for codes or names that look wrong. Rows with a note need the most attention.

## Rules used
- **Code = ID.** Every code is unique. Restocks reuse the original code (e.g. Oreo 2016 and 2022 are both `BY1604`), so each shoe appears once.
- **Reflective and non-reflective** versions are separate entries with their own codes.
- **Names** are the official colorway names. Pre-release nicknames are merged into the real shoe: "Jade Ash" is Salt (`HQ2060`), "Beige/Black" is Slate (`HP7870`), "Asriel" is Carbon (`FZ5000`), "Abez" is Natural (`FZ5246`), "MX Grey" is MX Dark Salt (`ID4811`).
- **Excluded:** kids/infant sizes, samples, friends-and-family pairs, the cancelled "Dark Green" (`DA9572`), and the original 350 (V1) models such as Pirate Black and Turtle Dove.
